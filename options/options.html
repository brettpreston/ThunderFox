Site exemptions

Add domains that ThunderFox should ignore entirely. A match applies to the listed domain and all of its subdomains.

Exempted sites

No sites are exempted right now. ThunderFox will run on every supported site.

    Reload already-open pages after editing the list. ThunderFox cannot hand a media element back to the browser once it has started processing it, so a reload is what makes a new exemption take full effect. Exempting a page also exempts everything it embeds in an iframe.
